You've seen programs where you make stuff and Hack Club ships you stickers.

What about a YSWS where you make the stickers and Hack Club ships them to you?

Introducing... You Stick, We Stick!

YOU SHIP: An original sticker design. Be creative!
WE SHIP: Hack Club ships your design as a real, high quality sticker!

In addition to recieving your designs shipped to you, you can also win Hack Club stickers of your choosing! The best stickers also have a chance to be added to a sticker pack for eternity!

Frequently Asked Questions:

Is this free?
yes
What are the rules?
Inappropriate or low-effort (including AI slop) stickers are not allowed. Apart from that, anything goes - have fun!
What prizes are there?
As prizes, you can recieve high quality stickers of your custom design, as well as official Hack Club stickers, shipped straight to you!
How does currency work?
In addition to recieving your designs shipped to you, each approved sticker earns "Pages" (or other undecided currency name), which can be spend on Hack Club stickers of your choosing!

This YSWS is not currently confirmed to run, but you can join #you-stick-we-stick on the Hack Club Slack to keep up with updates! In addition, make sure to RSVP above.
