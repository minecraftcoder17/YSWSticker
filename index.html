You've seen programs where you make stuff and Hack Club ships you stickers.
What about a ysws where you make the stickers and Hack Club ships them to you?
Introducing... You Stick, We Stick!

YOU SHIP: An original sticker design. Be creative!
WE SHIP: Hack Club ships your design as a real, high quality sticker!

In addition to recieving your designs shipped to you, each approved sticker earns "Sheets" (or other undecided currency name), which can be spend on Hack Club stickers of your choosing! The best stickers also have a chance to be added to a sticker pack for eternity!

Is this free?

yes

What are the rules?

Inappropriate or low-effort (including AI slop) stickers are not allowed. Apart from that, anything goes - have fun!

Is this confirmed yet?

This YSWS is not currently confirmed, but you can join #youstickwestick on the Hack Club Slack to keep up with updates! In addition, make sure to RSVP above.
